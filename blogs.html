Guide 01

How to build a remote-ready profile

A useful job profile makes it easier for an employer to understand what you can do without guessing. Start with a clear headline that names your role and relevant experience, for example: “Customer support associate with two years of CRM and email-support experience.”

Include evidence, not only labels

  • List concrete skills that match the work you want, such as Excel, Zendesk, Canva, Salesforce, written English, or video editing.
  • Add a short summary describing the kinds of problems you have solved and your preferred remote work arrangement.
  • Link to a portfolio, GitHub profile, LinkedIn page, Behance profile, or work samples when relevant.
  • Keep dates, job titles, and education information accurate and current.

Set realistic expectations

A strong profile can improve clarity; it cannot guarantee that an employer will view an application, invite you to interview, or make an offer. Tailor each application to the actual requirements of the role.

Guide 02

How to recognise job scams

Fraudsters often use urgency, high pay for very little work, and pressure to move the conversation outside trusted channels. Pause and verify before sharing information or making any payment.

Never pay an employer for registration, interview scheduling, training, equipment, background verification, a security deposit, an offer letter, or salary release. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords, card CVVs, or bank-login credentials.

Check before you apply

  • Search for the company’s official website and business contact information.
  • Compare the sender’s email address with the company’s official domain.
  • Be cautious if the role description is vague but asks for money or sensitive documents early.
  • Do not install unknown apps or remote-access tools at a recruiter’s request.
  • Report suspicious listings through the platform support channel.

Guide 03

How to write a clear remote job description

Clear job descriptions save time for both employers and applicants. They reduce irrelevant applications and help candidates decide whether a role is suitable before applying.

Cover the essentials

  • Use a specific title, such as “Remote Customer Support Executive” rather than “Work From Home Urgent Hiring.”
  • Describe main responsibilities, required skills, experience level, employment type, work hours, and location eligibility.
  • State pay range when possible, along with whether it is fixed, variable, or commission-based.
  • Explain the hiring steps honestly: screening, interview rounds, test assignment, and expected timeline.
  • Do not request payments, OTPs, or financial credentials from candidates at any stage.
